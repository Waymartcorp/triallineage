## Definition of complete

A TrialLineage page is complete only when:

- The endpoint is concrete and verified
- The title and card are endpoint-first
- Claims are source-backed
- Uncertainty is labeled
- The reverse-lineage structure is present
- Related concepts are secondary, not the organizing frame
- The page does not drift into a broad therapy-cluster explainer
