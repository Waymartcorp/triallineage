# Repeatable System Design

## What TrialLineage is

TrialLineage is designed as more than a collection of manually written science stories. It is being built as a repeatable public-science system.

## How it works

Each lineage begins with a concrete clinical endpoint — a Phase 1–3 trial, FDA approval, or regulatory milestone — and applies the same structured process:

1. Identify the endpoint
2. Summarize the clinical event
3. Trace backward through the enabling science
4. Surface key branch points and failed paths
5. Connect related concepts
6. Preserve source trails and uncertainty labels

## Why repeatability matters

Medical discovery is too large and fast-moving to explain one story at a time. TrialLineage aims to build an evidence-based engine that can continuously identify important clinical milestones and turn them into clear, reviewable public explanations of how science moves from basic discovery to human treatment.

## What this is not

The goal is not to replace scientific judgment. The goal is to make serious public science explanation more scalable, transparent, and durable.

## Relationship to infrastructure

The Production Room, intake scripts, news trigger pull, and lineage candidate scoring exist to support this repeatable process. They identify endpoints, gather structured data, and prepare the raw material for editorial treatment. The public Lineage Library is the output of this system — one endpoint at a time, each receiving the same structured reverse-lineage treatment.
